Saia is a sample representation agency with a private showroom and styling studio in New York City. We operate as a multi-functional fashion space designed to support both brands and stylists through a service-driven approach that prioritizes efficiency and elevated presentation, created to function as both a creative workspace and a client-facing environment.

Saia was started with the intention to create a streamlined and intentional experience tailored to the evolving needs of the fashion industry. We offer a highly curated setting where brands can engage directly with consumers and industry professionals, while stylists and creative teams are provided with the tools, support, and flexibility needed to execute projects seamlessly.

Founder & Creative Director

Lauren got her start as Drake's wardrobe assistant from 2017 to 2020, including a North American and European Tour.

She currently leads Nike's first Global Design Research Project Initiative, sourcing stylists worldwide to gather insights for international markets.

Most recently, Lauren served as a consultant for ESSX, where she conceived, built, and launched their Styling Studio — establishing relationships with celebrity stylists whose clients include Drake, Bad Bunny, Young Miko, Metro Boomin, Jhayco, Jim Jones, Queen Latifah, Peso Pluma, Tems, Sexyy Red, 21 Savage, Kai Cenat, Kim Kardashian, Coi Leray, Gunna, Jared Leto, NBA YoungBoy, Baby Keem, Stefon Diggs, Lil Nas X, and more.
