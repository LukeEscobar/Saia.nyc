About

We operate as a seasonless sample showroom and stylist concierge studio dedicated exclusively to strategic placement.

At the heart of our mission is the commitment to maximizing the lifecycle and return on investment of every sample we represent. Through strategic, high-impact placements, followed by curated sample sales, we transform each piece into both a publicity driver and a revenue-generating asset.

What We Offer Stylists

Our in-house stylist studio provides a dedicated space for fittings and style-outs, supported by concierge services tailored to the needs of professional stylists. Our style studio being located in close proximity to the full sample collections, provides stylists with convenient, immediate access for efficient style-outs and fittings.

What We Offer Brands

We provide archival sample placement that maintains brand visibility year-round. Our seasonless model ensures extended impact beyond the traditional fashion calendar.

Sample Sales

After each placement cycle, we host curated sample sales that give every piece its second return on investment. We offer our brand partners a dual value: elevated visibility and measurable financial return.
